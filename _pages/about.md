I am a first-year PhD student in Economics at [UC Berkeley](https://econ.berkeley.edu/), supported by the Chancellor's Fellowship. I am also a visiting economic researcher at [OpenAI](https://openai.com/). My research interests are in technology, innovation and organizations.

I graduated from the University of Chicago in 2024, with a bachelor's in Economics (Honors) and a master's in Computer Science. In college, I worked as a research assistant on projects about consumer finance (with [Scott Nelson](https://www.chicagobooth.edu/faculty/directory/n/scott-nelson)) and economic history (with [Thomas Sargent](http://www.tomsargent.com/) and [George Hall](https://people.brandeis.edu/~ghall/)), and interned on Citadel's Global Fixed Income team. From 2024–2026, I was a Predoctoral Research Associate at Harvard Business School, working with [Jesse Shapiro](https://www.economics.harvard.edu/people/jesse-shapiro).
